Humvee gunner/command

You are the commander of a humvee, deep in enemy territory. You are also the gunner. Realistic looking game where you just drive and manage your vehicle. Fight back against ambushes, call in airstrikes when you get in range and the weather is good. Choose your path on a hexagonal grid map. Pick up more vehicles and crew on the way. Like an anti-mine vehicle. You get an m2 machine gun, or a grenade launcher. Campaign style. Very fun.
