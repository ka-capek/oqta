humvee gunner / commander

you're the commander of a humvee, deep in enemy territory. you're also the gunner. a realistic-looking game where you just drive and manage your vehicle. fight back against ambushes, call in airstrikes when you're in range and the weather is good.

  • pick your path on a hexagonal grid map
  • pick up more vehicles and crew on the way, like an anti-mine vehicle
  • an m2 machine gun or a grenade launcher
  • campaign style

very fun.
