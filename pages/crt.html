Welcome to my personal site! I want to make this my own corner of the internet. Good vibes, clanker-free.

This page is the same page as the index, only it is sitting on a piece of curved glass. The edges bow out, the corners tuck in, the middle keeps its scale — and the text is still text: you can select it, follow the links, and read it with a screen reader.

How

CSS transforms are affine, so they cannot bend a straight line. The warp is an SVG feDisplacementMap, reached from CSS with filter: url(). Its map is mixed together from three gradients inside the filter, so nothing is loaded from disk and nothing is scripted.

No glow, no scanlines, no glare. Just the shape.
